## 4DF

Column 3a: accrued income before employee-paid taxes.
Column 3: paid income based on actual payment, without
subtracting taxes borne by the employee.

Regular salary paid in the prescribed period in the
following month can be attributed to the accrual month
under the applicable reporting rules.

The ERP control computes only a reconciliation candidate.
It requires independently verified payment-specific PIT
and levy withholding allocations.

Accrued PIT, PIT remitted to the budget, and PIT
withheld from a specific payment are not interchangeable.

No general statutory paid-gross attribution is approved
from a candidate arithmetic check alone.

## D6

D6 concerns legally defined special-service pension
periods and other applicable insurance periods.

If there are no relevant special-service workplaces,
D6 is not formed or filed under the applicable rule.

The current internal control does not independently
verify the complete legal eligibility and classification
of each special-service case.

A zero count is accepted only with separately provided
eligibility evidence verification. This function does
not itself inspect that external evidence.

# Phase 13.14 — D6 live HR binding and 4DF withholding gap

## D6 live HR binding

Candidate evidence is checked against the actual PostgreSQL
company, employee, employment contract and reporting period.

A positive binding means that the contract belongs to the
employee and company, and that the requested dates fall
within the reporting period and contract term.

It does not establish special-service eligibility or
approve the candidate's eight-digit normative basis code.

## 4DF payment-specific withholding

The existing PostgreSQL payment verifier establishes:
- posted payroll disbursement and amount,
- employee/calculation identity,
- actual payment date,
- posted PIT and military-levy remittance evidence.

Tax remittance is not equivalent to employee withholding
attributable to a specific salary payment.

The supplementary gap audit makes this distinction
explicit and blocks gross-paid approval.

## Remaining statutory requirements

- Independent payment-specific withholding source
- Effective-dated 4DF income mapping and corrections
- Approved D6 basis-code registry and HR special-service proof
- Final Phase 13.15 full regression and acceptance

## Regulatory limits

The actual NET salary disbursement is not automatically the
statutory GROSS PAID income amount.

No gross paid attribution is approved by this module.

D6 source coverage does not constitute authoritative
normative classification or official report approval.

Even a valid posted-payment journal does not independently
establish an approved statutory income category.

All official generation remains BLOCKED.
